Our Love Story

We met in high school, two kids who had no idea they were about to meet their person. We fell in love fast, but when COVID hit, we said goodbye and went our separate ways to college, each carrying fond memories of what we'd shared.

Years later, we both found ourselves back in El Paso, each dropping off a younger sibling at our old high school. It was like no time had passed. We started talking again, and before long, we were dating again.

That Thanksgiving, Aldo proposed right in the middle of dinner at Isabella's family table, surrounded by tears, laughter, and cheers. We spent the summer in Seattle celebrating, and now we can't wait to marry the love of our lives with all of you there.
